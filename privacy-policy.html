Privacy policy

Here we explain what data we process when you visit the BikeRc website or write to us, why we do it and what rights you have.

1. Data controller

The controller of personal data is BIKERC Prosta Spółka Akcyjna, with its registered office at ul. Mieszka I 48/50, 35-303 Rzeszów, Poland, tax ID (NIP) 8133919829, National Court Register (KRS) no. 0001121546.

For any matter concerning personal data, you can write to kontakt@bikerc.pl.

2. Contact by email

When you send us a message, we process your email address, your name (if you provide it), the content of the message and any other data you include in it.

  • Purpose: replying to your message and carrying on correspondence.
  • Legal basis: our legitimate interest, namely being able to reply to your message (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). If you write about a service, for example a fitting, the basis is also taking steps prior to entering into a contract (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Retention: for as long as the correspondence continues, and then until any claims become time-barred or until you successfully object.

Providing your data is voluntary, but without an email address we cannot reply.

3. Visiting the website

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Each time the site is visited, the server records technical connection data, including the IP address, date and time, and browser information. This data is used to keep the site running and secure (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). We do not use it to identify visitors.

4. Cookies and statistics

The website does not store cookies and does not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools. Fonts, images and videos are hosted directly on the site and are not loaded from third-party servers.

5. Recipients of data

  • GitHub, Inc. (USA), the website hosting provider.
  • home.pl S.A. (Szczecin, Poland), the email service provider.

Data may be transferred to the United States in connection with the hosting of the website. This takes place on the basis of the European Commission's adequacy decision (EU-US Data Privacy Framework) or standard contractual clauses approved by the Commission.

6. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • access your data and receive a copy of it,
  • have your data rectified,
  • have your data erased,
  • restrict processing,
  • data portability,
  • object to processing based on legitimate interest.

To exercise these rights, write to kontakt@bikerc.pl. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych, ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warsaw, Poland).

We do not make automated decisions and we do not profile people who write to us.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example if we add a contact form or statistics tools. The current version is always available on this page.

Effective from 30 October 2026.
